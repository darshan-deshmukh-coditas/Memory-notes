  What works
  - ingest reads the notes folder, resolves owners and due dates, merges

    --due-before and --json options, and AND-combined filters.
  - done <id> updates the store only. The source notes are not modified.
  - followup <owner> / --all creates one Gmail draft per owner through the MCP
    connector and never sends. It handles repeat detection, --force, and exit
    codes 1, 2 and 3.
